# Core150 Manual Spot-Check Record

Recorded: 2026-07-23

The authors report that both authors manually inspected the same random sample
of 10 queries from Core150 and judged the sampled cases to be of high quality.

This was a qualitative spot check. It was not:

- a manual review of all 150 Core150 queries;
- a blinded or independently assigned annotation study;
- a source of formal inter-reviewer agreement statistics; or
- a basis for describing Core150 or Full1000 as human validated.

Permitted manuscript wording:

> A random sample of 10 Core150 queries was manually inspected by both authors
> and judged qualitatively to be of high quality.
